Glossary · The terms you'll see on every invoice
Gross Food Sales
Food sales + every tax bucket. Top-line revenue figure.
Food Sales
Pre-tax revenue from food. Commission is calculated on this (after refunds).
Sales Tax — Restaurant Remit
What the restaurant owes the state. If $0.00, Fooda is handling it.
Sales Tax — Fooda Remit
Sales tax Fooda is sending to the state on the restaurant's behalf.
Commission + Event Fee
Fooda's cut: 20–25% of food sales after refunds, plus a $5 flat event fee.
Processing Fees
Card-network costs. In-person 1.75% + $0.25. Online/mobile 3.5% + $0.15.
Section Reference
Internal Fooda ID for an event line (e.g., NV0000000). Look it up in Snappea.
Deposit / ACH Date
When Fooda releases payment via ACH. Funds availability depends on the bank.
